Experience

Hello, my name is Sara Abi Ghosn. I want to share my personal experience to raise awareness about the importance of staying hydrated and the serious health problems that can result from not drinking enough water. During the summer of 2024, I was doing a lot of sports but often forgot to drink enough water. Even when I went to the beach and spent hours in the heat, I sometimes avoided drinking simply because I felt too lazy to get some water. Eventually, this unhealthy habit contributed to a serious case of gastroenteritis, accompanied by a very high fever. I became so dehydrated that I needed intravenous fluids to replace the water and nutrients my body had lost. The illness was severe, and it took me about a month and a half to recover and return to eating normally. This experience taught me that drinking enough water is essential, especially when exercising or spending time in hot weather.
